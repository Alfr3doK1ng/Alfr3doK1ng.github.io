SC.02

Logline

Fade in — Int. a desk in the Bay Area — Night

I’m Alfred — an engineer who lives where models meet real people. Right now I’m at OpenArt, democratizing art creation by turning a single conversation into a finished film. Before that I built work agents at Moveworks, co-founded a startup that searched TikTok like Google, and spent college backtesting my way through markets. These days I think about agent harnesses, evals, RL for planning and tool use, and agents that improve themselves.
